Welcome to Our Environment

The environment includes air, water, land, plants, animals, and all living things. It provides us with everything we need to live.

Importance of the Environment

  • Provides clean air and water.
  • Gives us food and natural resources.
  • Provides homes for animals and plants.
  • Maintains balance in nature.

How Can We Protect the Environment?

  1. Plant more trees.
  2. Reduce, reuse, and recycle waste.
  3. Avoid throwing rubbish on the ground.
  4. Save water and electricity.
  5. Keep our surroundings clean.

Environmental Problems

Problem Solution
Air Pollution Use cleaner transport.
Deforestation Plant and protect trees.
Plastic Waste Reuse and recycle plastic.
Water Pollution Keep rivers and lakes clean.
